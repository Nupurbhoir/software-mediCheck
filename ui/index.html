System Name: MediTrack Enterprise EMR | Organization: Aarogya Healthcare (27 Facilities)

  3. FR-03 (Mandatory Allergy Recording): Direct preventative requirement for the 310 historical prescribing incidents.
  4. FR-04 (Outpatient SOAP Notes): Signed electronic encounter notes with immutable audit addendums.
  5. FR-05 (Inpatient Bed Census): Ward allocation, transfer workflows, and bed status synchronization.

  10. FR-10 (Discharge Summary): Structured discharge summaries with reconciled medication instructions.
  11. FR-11 (Immutable Audit Trail): Chained SHA-256 Merkle logging for all read, write, and override events.
  12. FR-12 (Legacy Data Quarantine): 12-stage ETL with automated isolation of the 168,000 (7%) problematic legacy records.

8 Measurable Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs):

  • NFR-01 (Security): TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest, OAuth2 / JWT authentication with mandatory MFA.

  • NFR-03 (Availability): Greater than or equal to 99.857% continuous availability over rolling 12 months.
  • NFR-04 (Usability): 95% of nurse cohort achieves operational competency within 20 minutes of instruction.
  • NFR-05 (Reliability): MTBF greater than or equal to 1,400 hours continuous operation without crash.
  • NFR-06 (Performance): Patient search latency less than or equal to 1.5 seconds under 1,200 concurrent users.
  • NFR-07 (Maintainability): MTTR less than or equal to 2.0 hours with decoupled asynchronous billing adapter.
  • NFR-08 (Data Integrity): Defect Removal Efficiency (DRE) greater than or equal to 92% prior to go-live.

2. Sequence Diagram: Prescribe Drug with Allergy Check

Doctor accesses CPOE -> Enters drug & dosage -> CPOE queries DrugSafetyEngine -> DrugSafetyEngine checks patient allergy history -> If Fatal, returns HARD STOP modal -> If Safe, issues Rx and emits PrescriptionIssuedEvent to message broker.

3. Software Architecture: Prescription & Billing Decoupling

Clinical CPOE operates on an autonomous Port-and-Adapter architecture. When a prescription is signed, the CPOE service persists the clinical record and emits an asynchronous PrescriptionIssuedEvent to RabbitMQ/Kafka. The detached BillingAdapter consumes the event to charge patient accounts without holding clinical prescribing hostage to billing downtime.
